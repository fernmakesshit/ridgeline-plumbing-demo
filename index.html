Services

What we fix.

Most jobs are done in one visit. If a part has to be ordered, you hear about it before we leave, along with the day we're coming back.

01

Leak repair

Under sinks, inside walls, slab leaks. We find the source, fix it, and show you the part that failed.

02

Water heaters

Tank and tankless, repaired or replaced. Permits pulled when required and the old unit hauled off.

03

Drain cleaning

Kitchen, tub and main line clogs cleared. Camera inspection when the same drain keeps backing up.

04

Whole home repipe

Old galvanized or polybutylene lines replaced with PEX or copper. Most homes take 2 to 3 days.

05

Toilets and fixtures

Running toilets, dripping faucets, disposals, shower valves. Installed or repaired, your parts or ours.

06

Small business plumbing

Restaurants, salons, offices and shops. Early morning and after close scheduling so you stay open.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you charge to come out?

Yes, a flat visit fee. If you go ahead with the repair, it comes off the total.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our California contractor license number is at the top of this page and printed on every invoice.

Can you come tonight?

For burst pipes, sewage backups or no water, call and we'll tell you how fast we can get there. Everything else gets scheduled during regular hours.

Do you warranty your work?

Labor is covered for one year. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.

How do I pay?

Card, check, Zelle or cash when the job is done.
