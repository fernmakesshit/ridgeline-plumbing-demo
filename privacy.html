Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

This page explains what information Ridgeline Plumbing collects through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you fill out the quote form, we collect your name, phone number, city and anything you write in the details box. If you call or email, we keep the number or address you contact us from.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request, schedule work, send invoices and follow up on a job. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Service providers we use to run the business, like our scheduling, payment and email tools. They can only use it to provide those services to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to count visits. We don't use advertising trackers.

Your California privacy rights

California residents can ask what personal information we have about them, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email or call us using the contact info on the home page and we'll handle it within 45 days.

How long we keep it

Job records are kept as long as needed for warranty, tax and licensing requirements. Quote requests that don't turn into a job are deleted after 12 months.
